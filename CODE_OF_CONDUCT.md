# Code of Conduct

PurpleDragonFlipperStudio welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. Everyone participating in issues, pull requests, discussions, and project spaces is expected to help maintain a respectful and inclusive community.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and code respectfully; accept constructive feedback.
- Respect people's identities, privacy, boundaries, and accessibility needs.
- Give credit for others' work and help newcomers participate.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, sexual harassment, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without permission are unacceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns to the repository owner, [bubblegump30](https://github.com/bubblegump30). Use a private contact method the owner lists on their profile for sensitive reports; do not post private information in a public issue. If no private contact is listed, request a private reporting channel without including sensitive details.

The maintainer will review reports fairly, respect reporter privacy where possible, and decide on an appropriate response. Responses may include removing content, requesting corrective action, issuing a warning, or temporarily or permanently restricting participation. Severe or repeated misconduct may result in an immediate ban.

## Scope

This policy applies within project spaces and when someone represents the project publicly. It does not authorize harassment or retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.
